Welcome to Tawakkal Exports

Strategically based in Panvel, Maharashtra, India — adjacent to India's premier maritime logistics gateways — Tawakkal Exports stands as a highly reliable, dual-channel merchant trading institution. We optimize supply chains across two primary global divisions: the large-scale bulk export of premium Indian agricultural products and commodities, and the direct wholesale import of high-demand consumer appliances, industrial components, and lifestyle necessities.
